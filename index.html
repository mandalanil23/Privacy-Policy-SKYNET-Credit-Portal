Privacy Policy: SKYNET Credit Portal

Effective date: 1 November 2026. App: SKYNET Credit Portal (com.skynetww.creditportal)

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SKYNET Worldwide Express (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the SKYNET Credit Portal Android application (the “App”). By installing and using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

a) Information you provide

  • Account and login details, such as your user ID or username and password, or other credentials issued to you for the portal.
  • Business and account details you enter or that are shown to you in the portal, such as customer or franchise details, credit balance, transaction and billing information.
  • Information you send us when you contact support, such as your name, phone number and email address.

b) Information collected automatically

  • Basic device and technical information such as device model, Android version, app version, language and network status, used to keep the App working properly.
  • Diagnostic information such as crash and error data, if enabled.

c) Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your precise or approximate location.
  • We do not access your contacts, SMS, call logs, photos, camera, microphone or files.
  • We do not collect payment card numbers or bank passwords through the App itself.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information to:

  • Authenticate you and give you access to your account on the SKYNET Credit Portal.
  • Show your credit, balance and account information, and keep it up to date.
  • Send you notifications about your account, such as balance alerts and important updates.
  • Maintain, secure and improve the App, and fix errors.
  • Prevent fraud and misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Sharing of Information

We share information only in these cases:

  • Service providers: trusted companies that host our servers or provide technical support, and who are bound to protect your data.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, court order or a government authority, or to protect our rights, users or the public.
  • Business transfer: if our business is merged, acquired or sold, your information may be transferred, and we will tell you.

We do not share your data with advertisers, and the App does not show ads.

6. Data Security

Data is sent between the App and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The App does not allow unencrypted (HTTP) traffic. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service, meet legal, accounting and audit requirements, and resolve disputes. After that, we delete or anonymise it.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to close your account, by contacting us at the address below. You can turn off notifications at any time in your Android settings. Uninstalling the App stops all further collection through the App. Data already held on our servers is deleted as described in Section 7, or on request.

Account and data deletion: to request deletion of your account and related data, email [your-email@example.com] with the subject “Delete my account” from your registered email or phone number. We will respond within [30] days.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for business users and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Third-Party Links and Services

The App may show content from or link to websites operated by others. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.
